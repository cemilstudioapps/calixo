CALIXO Support

For help with a CALIXO application, a privacy question, bug report or account or service issue, email cemil.studioapps@gmail.com.

Reporting a bug

If useful, you may include:

  • the CALIXO application name and app version;
  • your iOS version and device model;
  • a short description of the issue.

Do not send passwords, banking credentials or other sensitive authentication information by email. Do not send financial documents or private records by email.
