CALIXO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Local application data

CALIXO applications are designed around local-first processing. In CALIXO VAULT, personal financial records and documents are intended to be stored and processed on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that requires an external service. This does not mean that every present or future feature operates only on-device.

Optional AI features

Some optional CALIXO Plus features may use an external AI provider. For the planned CALIXO VAULT Plus Analysis feature, CALIXO calculates financial figures before AI processing and intends to transmit only minimized financial aggregates needed to explain the requested analysis.

This Analysis path is designed to exclude original documents, document images and PDFs, OCR content, bank credentials and account numbers, individual bank transactions, names, postal addresses and free-form personal notes.

AI-generated text is treated as an explanatory proposal. CALIXO validates outputs before display; rejected responses can fall back to local deterministic analysis. This feature is not currently active for real users. AI functionality may be activated only when applicable privacy and operational controls are ready.

Service providers and data minimization

Optional online functionality may rely on third-party infrastructure providers. Provider processing may be subject to their applicable terms and privacy and security controls. CALIXO aims to transmit only the information needed for an online feature you request.

Your choice

Optional AI functionality is intended to require your choice or consent before use. Where applicable, you can continue using local functionality without choosing an optional online AI feature.

Younger users

Some CALIXO applications may be suitable for younger users depending on the specific product and feature. Features involving financial guidance or online AI processing may be subject to additional safeguards, age restrictions or consent requirements before public availability.
